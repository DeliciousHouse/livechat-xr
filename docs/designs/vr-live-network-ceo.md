## Vision

### 10x Check
A "who's live" list is passive. The 10x version turns discovery into actions small streamers take together: in a squad game like Population: ONE, two or three small streamers playing the same match expose each audience to the others live, for roughly twice the effort of the list.

### Platonic Ideal
Before going live, a streamer opens Discord and sees which Pop1 streamers their size are live, whose squad needs a player, and the quietest hour to stand out tonight. Viewers who love the game get pinged when a small stream starts. After the stream, the streamer sees which raid brought viewers and follows, and which moments are worth clipping.
